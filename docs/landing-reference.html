    How to check a KredoFi note

    Central banks print money with details that are hard to fake. We built every loan the same way. Keep scrolling, or hover the note to look through the loupe.

    1. The amount

      $100 is your limit. The rules set it, not a loan officer. Repay this one on time and your next note says $250.

    2. The letter

      Your band, from A to D. Like the colour-shifting ink on a real note, it changes as you move. It's the only score that goes on-chain.

    3. The serial number

      A keyed fingerprint of your bank account. One account can back only one borrower, ever.

    4. The thread

      Your name, encrypted and woven in. It opens for our recovery partner only if you never repay.

    5. The watermark

      Proof that the scoring ran inside Intel TDX with the published code. Anyone can check it against the transaction on NEAR.
